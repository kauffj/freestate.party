The Founding Compact

in witness whereof we set our hands

The Party is a private club in which you participate voluntarily. A central thesis of the Party is that Membership selection is as much, or more important, than written rules. Thus, Rule zero:

Rule 0.Act such that the club need not add more Rules.

Civilization is not maintained by written rules alone, but by their fusion with culture and interpretation. Three additional founding Rules:

Rule 1.Cherish liberty, family, truth, virtue and enterprise and reinforce them in your family, among club Members, in your community, and in New Hampshire.
Rule 2.Cooperate; resolve conflicts internally.
Rule 3.Respect confidentiality and privacy.

The Party also makes Promises to Members:

i.The Party will strive not to add more Rules. It will strive to remove incompatible Members.
ii.All Party Officers are Members, and will be held to the same standards as Members. No one is above the Rules.
iii.All Party decisions regarding Rules violations, Rules changes, and Membership removals will be communicated to Members via the Party application, with full, uncensored Member comment.
iv.The Party will improve your ability to accomplish Rule One.

in witness whereof we set our hands
